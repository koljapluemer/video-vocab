Let's bring a bit of diversity in the snippet exercise flow.
Establish a pattern where it's easy to add exercise templates, and give them weights for random picking.
For now, establish the following templates:

## Look for vocabulary 

Weight: 5

Basically the current pattern: Show three vocab in the beginning and instruct to see how they're used.
After  snippet watching, change the question to: How was the vocabulary that you just saw used?

## Meaning

Weight: 2

A very simple one: Pre-instruction is only "Try to understand the gist of what was said".
After watching, textarea with question: What did you understand?

## Repeat

Weight: 2

Pre-instruction: Try to memorize the words and the sound of a part of the following video, then repeat it.
After watching, we need a little audio recorder component, allowing the user to record themselves, listen to their recording, possibly re-record.

## Vibe

Weight: 1

Pre-instruction: Listen and watch for gestures, non-verbal language, tone and mood
Post-instruction: Textarea with "What did you notice?"
